Motorsport Career v3.0.2 - Garage, Used Market & Race Weather

- Fixed runtime error in used-car listings: the hashing helper was previously private to another module. This prevented used-market and later UI from rendering.
- Fixed used-car purchases: technical-condition initialization no longer references a private helper from another module.
- Used Vehicle Market always renders at the top of My Garage, including locked offers.
- Sold vehicles are removed from active technical condition cards; historical condition snapshots are retained in vehicle28.retired.
- Repurchasing a model creates a fresh condition record, without restoring the previous owner's wear. Used purchases start with the listed condition.
- Weather Center is mounted within the Race Weekend, below its progress steps and visible during Briefing, Preparation and Race.
- Free weekends can select the circuit and event date directly from Weather Center. Forecasts are informational and only available for mapped real circuits in the forecast window.
- PWA cache bumped to v302. No change to account, contract, race results or existing wallet transactions.

Validation: JavaScript syntax and ZIP integrity. Browser interaction could not be tested because local browser navigation is blocked by administrator policy.
