Motorsport Career v3.0.4 - Vehicle Management Redesign
- Three tabs: My Vehicles, Vehicle Market, Technical Center.
- Removes legacy pre-defined purchase grid, keeps original ownership state and purchase/sale logic.
- Kart can be purchased from Vehicle Market during Academy.
- New/used markets are together; used offers use named catalogue cars rather than generic Gr. classes.
- Legacy generic cars can be converted for free to a named model of the same category, preserving technical condition.
- Team-supplied vehicles shown separately from owned cars.
- No automatic reset or balance changes.
- Weather Center and existing game systems unchanged.
